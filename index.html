What is psych?

psych is a revision site for IB Psychology. It keeps revision material and the studies behind it, exam-style questions, key terms for the course concepts, and a prompt for AI-assisted marking in one place.

It exists because revision usually means digging through a folder of documents to find the one study that fits the question in front of you. I am still adding to it, and improving what is already here, as I work through the course.
